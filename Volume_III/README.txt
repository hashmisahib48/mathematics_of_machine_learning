The Mathematics of Machine Learning: An Illustrated Guide to Every scikit-learn Algorithm
Volume III: Unsupervised Learning, Data Preparation and Model Selection -- companion files
Dr. Mudassar Hussain Hashmi

ch00/         the refresher (Volumes I and II in Brief); chNN/ for Chapters 36-59
chNN/code/    the programs of the chapter (run them from inside the chapter folder or its code folder, as their paths require;
              code/run_all.sh reruns them all)
chNN/verify/  the outputs those programs printed, and the numbers behind the figures, examples and problem answers (.json)
chNN/fig/     the scripts that draw every figure, equation image and problem illustration of the chapter
lib/          shared figure style (figstyle.py) and equation renderer (mkeq.py) used by the fig scripts

Tested with Python 3.13, scikit-learn 1.9.1, NumPy 2.5, SciPy 1.18, Matplotlib 3.11, pandas 3.0.
All data sets ship with scikit-learn or are generated by the programs; no download is needed.
Timings printed in the book depend on the machine and will differ from your own runs.
